📢 Mark your calendars for the annual Geomatics Day at TU Delft! This exciting afternoon event brings together students, faculty, companies, and alumni to celebrate achievements and explore opportunities in the geomatics field.

## What to Expect

Geomatics Day offers an engaging afternoon filled with:

- Synthesis project presentations by 2nd year students
- Showcases of the top 3 theses from 2025
- Company booths featuring industry leaders
- Networking opportunities with alumni
- Insights into current trends and future opportunities in geomatics

## Event Details

- **Date:** November 7, 2025
- **Time:** 13:00-17:00
- **Location:** TU Delft, Netherlands
- **Who should attend:** All geomatics students (1st-year attendance is mandatory!)

## Important Note for 1st Year Students

All 1st-year students are expected to attend this event. The schedule has been carefully coordinated to ensure no exams conflict with Geomatics Day, so there's no reason to miss this valuable opportunity!
