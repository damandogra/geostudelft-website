# INTERGEO 2025

**INTERGEO**, the world's leading trade fair for **geodesy, geoinformation and land management**, will open its doors in **Frankfurt am Main, Germany** from **October 7 to 9, 2025**.

---

## Key Areas Covered

- Geoinformation
- Surveying
- GIS
- Remote sensing
- Drone technology
- Smart cities
- Climate protection
- Sustainable urban development

---

## Significance

INTERGEO is the **central platform** for discussing and promoting forward-looking solutions to global challenges such as:

- Climate change
- Urbanization
- Resource management

---

## Social Impact

The social significance of INTERGEO lies in **promoting innovations** that make our living space:

- Smarter
- More sustainable
- More liveable

This is where the **minds that use data and technologies** to better understand and actively shape our world meet — **for the people of today and future generations**.
