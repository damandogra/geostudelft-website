Every year, GEOS organizes a **3-day trip to INTERGEO** together with Geomatics students. In 2026 we're heading to **Munich, Germany** from **15 to 17 September**.

INTERGEO is the world's leading trade fair for **geodesy, geoinformation and land management**. It's a great opportunity to:

- See how the industry and real-world projects work in the field of Geomatics
- Discover the latest in surveying, GIS, remote sensing, drones and smart cities
- Network with professionals and talk to cutting-edge companies in the field
