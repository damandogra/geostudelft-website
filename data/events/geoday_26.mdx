📢 Save the date: Geomatics Day 2026 is on **Friday 6 November**! Geomatics Day is the annual research & networking event of the MSc Geomatics for the Built Environment programme at TU Delft.

## What to Expect

- Synthesis project results: students presenting real-world work done in partnership with organisations like Rijkswaterstaat, Deltares and Haskoning
- Company pitches from leading geo-innovation partners
- A session on AI and education in Geomatics
- The announcement of the Geomatics Best Thesis Award 2026
- Drinks and networking with students, alumni and professionals from across the geo-information field

## Event Details

- **Date:** Friday, November 6, 2026
- **Time:** 13:00-17:15
- **Location:** Berlage Rooms, Faculty of Architecture and the Built Environment, TU Delft
- **Who should attend:** Everybody is welcome! Current students, alumni, or anyone curious about how geomatics is shaping the built environment

⚠️ **Registration is required.** Please sign up before you come.
